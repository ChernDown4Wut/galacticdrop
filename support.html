❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play Galactic Drop?

Drop planets onto matching colors below. Each correct match increases your score. If anything crosses the line at the bottom, the round ends. Reach exactly 10 points to win!

Is Galactic Drop free?

Yes — completely free with no subscriptions. No in-app purchases either.

Does the game have ads?

No third-party ads. Just pure gameplay.

My game crashed. What should I do?

Try restarting the app. If the problem continues, email us with your device model and iOS version, and we'll investigate.

Can I suggest a feature or report a bug?

Absolutely! Email us anytime. We love feedback from players.

📱 Report a Problem

If you encounter a bug, please include:

  • Your device (iPhone/iPad model)
  • iOS version
  • What you were doing when the issue occurred
  • A screenshot (if possible)
